Guideline sources

External links to professional and guideline-publisher references for clinician review. The workspace does not fetch, interpret, or use these links to generate diagnoses or recommendations for a case.

Not for clinical use. This workspace has no validated diagnostic engine. Its score calculations have not been independently validated and must not guide patient care. Guideline recommendations change, vary by jurisdiction, and depend on the patient context; external links are references only and are not applied to individual cases.

Blueprint registries · coverage and status

Each specialty module lists an external professional or guideline reference. Open the source and verify the current version, jurisdiction, scope, and patient-specific applicability yourself. These links are reference-only: they do not power diagnosis generation. Specialty modules are documentation scaffolds; this registry provides no diagnostic algorithms, evidence retrieval, or recommendations. Any draft review controls in the workspace are transient and are not persisted.
